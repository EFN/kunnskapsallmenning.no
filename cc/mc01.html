OPEN MONEY
THE PROBLEM WITH MONEY:
      it can go almost anywhere -- and it does
      it's scarce -- there's only so much of it
      it comes from somewhere else -- we don't make it here

THE OPEN MONEY SOLUTION:
      We make the money we need and keep it here:
      Local currencies -- Sectorial currencies -- Community currencies

Money is only information -- a ticket system to measure how we value other things, including our time. Money is like inches and gallons (centimeters and kilograms).

Community money works just like a virtual bank or credit union. It is a mutual credit network where you can only use your account to trade with other members.
