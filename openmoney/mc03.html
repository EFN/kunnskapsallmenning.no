PAYSWARM
An open web platform for voluntary micropayments, enabling web browsers and web devices to perform Universal Web Payment, eliminating or reducing the friction between you and the people you want to reward on the Web.

Micropayments refers to the ability to automatically distribute payments in increments of up to 1/100,000th of a cent to anybody listed in PaySwarm digital contracts.

Payswarm enables copyright-aware, peer-to-peer digital media distribution, competition between distributors, customer choice between distributors, enables the customer to become a distributor, enabling "resale" as an additional download source for the content, is a non-proprietary technology, and endeavors to make every web browser and Internet device into a point-of-sale and redistribution for digital content.

Payswarm is a peer-to-peer "filesharing" version of money, meaning it is an independent currency not tied to any country's currency and with untraceable transactions.
