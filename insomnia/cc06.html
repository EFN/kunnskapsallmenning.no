C/NC AND THE COLLECTIVE RIGHTS SOCIETIES

Some problem-inducing facts:
  1. 92 % of Kopinor's revenue comes from "non-commercial" use (in educational institutions, public libraries and churches/denominations)
  2. By law, CC precedes the agreements and licenses of the rights societies.
  3. The use of CC bypasses and may reduce the amount (and redistribution) of money collected by the rights societies.
  4. Kopinor is not a member organization, so cannot make individual and binding agreements wrt CC.
All this complicates the distinction between "commercial" and "non-commercial" use, and leads to the distinction being contested or negotiated in different ways.

Furthermore: Members of TONO give up the copyrights to their own music, and therefore do not have the right to license it, e.g. with a CC license. Due to much discussion and complaints, TONO is considering to adjust to and allow non-commercial CC licenses.

So do we go for an American definition of C/NC, a Norwegian or European definition, or a specially tailored definition (like in the Netherlands or Denmark?

CC Norway has for the time being opted for the American model, as we don't have anything to offer Kopinor today.
