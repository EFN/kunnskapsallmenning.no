OTHER PURPOSES OF CREATIVE COMMONS
  1. Finding and retrieving works with any specific or all free licenses, in any jurisdiction, via searches. Improve searches through standardized metadata.

  2. Establish and maintain a common global marking system (icons and metadata) which is independent of and transcends language and cultural backgrounds.

  3. Increase people's knowledge and awareness of intellectual and moral rights.

  4. A conservative purpose: limited PR by making a small part of one's works available freely, in order to sell copies or access to the rest (majority) of the works.
