ANOTHER CHALLENGING CONCEPT:
MORAL RIGHTS

Moral rights have weak and little protection in the US.
A non-American CC license cannot introduce stronger protection of moral rights of the work in the US, for two reasons:
  1. CC International would never accept that.
  2. Even if they did accept it, that doesn't mean that the US suddenly would begin to respect moral rights.
In Norwegian law, moral rights consist of three non-transferable, everlasting rights (ÅVL § 3). The right to:
1. Attribution by name (author credit right)
2. Non-offensive accessibility and adaptation
3. Disassociation
Attribution and disassociation are unproblematic in CC - they are explicitly protected (for all CC licences). The problem is non-offensive accessibility and adaptation. CCi wants licensors to renounce this right. That is not possible in Norway, so what happens then?
A non-binding statement of intent not to sue.
