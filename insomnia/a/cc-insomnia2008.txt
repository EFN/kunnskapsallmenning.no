Insomnia, a festival for techno and future culture (www.insomniafestival.no)
and NORUT , an applied research institute (www.norut.no) shall arrange a
one-day seminar about digital rights, intellectual property rights,
CreativeCommons and digital production and distribution in the media
business (music, film and video, gaming etc.) .  The seminar is also an
arrangement in an international project; NoCry ~V Northern Creative Youth ~V
with participants from Norway, Sweden, Finland, the Faeroe Islands Scotland
and Northern Ireland, which a.o. focuses on such  digital production and
distribution.  The seminar will take place in TVIBIT (a youth club) in
Tromsø, Friday October 10th. .  The outline for the project is as follows: 

1	Large Screen intro by Larry Lessig: . How creativity is being
strangled by the law 
2.	From music industry:  Digital rights as seen from music companies
point of view  (Not confirmed yet) 
3.	Torgeir Waterhouse, IKT-Norge : How to deal with fileshare 
4.	Thomas Gramstad (Creative Commons Norway) : About Creative Commons

5.	Thomas Nordtvedt, Forbrukerrådet : What will the users require in
the future? 
6.	Per Martinsen, (artist: Frost, Mental Overdrive, etc.): Experience
with digital distribution seen from an artist point of view. 
7.	Researcher Njaal Borch (NORUT): Digital distribution in the future:
Free or controlled? 

Our goal with the seminar is to have a constructive dialogue about the
challenges raised; how will it in the future be possible for creative people
to be creative, and how will it be possible for creative people to earn
money on ther creativity; in a paradigm where much of the content may be
distibuted free on the net? Our purpose is not to have an antagonistic
collision between different paradigms, but rather have a constructive and
fruitful dialog between people with different point of views. The invited
speakers are therefore people who have a good experience and understanding
of problems and possibilities. The participants at the seminar will be NoCry
project partners, young creative people from Tromsø, people in media
business, business developers, newspapers etc. 


We therefore ask you if it will be possible to have a short speech, approx.
20 min. about the topics described here. The seminar will last in the time
between 10.00 to 15.00 on October 10th.  It will be possible to return to
Oslo the same day, but we invite all speakers to experience also the
festival itself. We will cover travel and subsistence, and we have reserved
hotel rooms for speakers and other participants. In addition we will pay a
modest fee to the speakers.

After the seminar it will be possible to attend Insomniafestival and listen
to different artists and experience different cultural events (seminars,
exhibitions etc) It will be a hot weekend in Tromsø! The festival is an
important meeting place for technoculture in Tromsø.  The program for the
festival is available at www.insomniafestival.no.
