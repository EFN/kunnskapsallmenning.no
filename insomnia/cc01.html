The primary purpose of Creative Commons:
To create commons (public property areas) of "Free cultural works"

Definition of Free Cultural Works:
(From: https://freedomdefined.org/Definition)
(In Norwegian: https://freedomdefined.org/Definition/Nb)

  1. The freedom to use the work and enjoy the benefits of using it
  2. The freedom to study the work and to apply knowledge acquired from it
  3. The freedom to make and redistribute copies, in whole or in part, of the information or expression
  4. The freedom to make changes and improvements, and to distribute derivative works

Goal:
The four freedoms should be available to everybody, anytime and anywhere. They should not be limited by context or how the work is being used. Creativity is to use an existing resource in a new or unusual way.
