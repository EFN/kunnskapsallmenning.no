BUILDING ABUNDANCE-BASED BUSINESS MODELS
Or:
HOW DO YOU MAKE MONEY WITH
FREE FLOW AND ABUNDANCE?

CC-licenses can aid you the musician or creator with:
  1. Building business models for an abundance-based economy (abundance of copies and abundance of works).
  2. The competition for mindshare/mindspace.
This elimination of scarcity of cultural works and knowledge is a huge leap forward. It represents an unprecedented potential for social progress, freedom and democratic participation.

At the same time it creates new scarcities of attention or mind-based scarcities.

This new world of mind-based scarcities is a result of and actively requires an abundance of copies (and work variants/work families) of your work in order to succeed in making your work and yourself visible and thereby improve your ability to earn money on your work.
