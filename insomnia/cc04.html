CHOOSING A CREATIVE COMMONS LICENSE
You choose a CC license for your work by answering the questions on the license web page:
https://creativecommons.org/license/
(Norwegian version:
https://creativecommons.org/license/?lang=no)
These questions or choices give rise to 6 different CC licenses.

From the strictest of these licenses we see that CC leads to a "minimum requirement" or a minimum amount of freedom, which is the right to copy and distribute non-commercially the whole work without any changes.

Earlier there has been a few more CC licences than these 6, and there may still be some works around covered by older licenses.
