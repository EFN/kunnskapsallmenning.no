THORNY CONCEPTS:
Commercial and Non-Commercial
Commercial = For profit? (Ref. Norwegian law, ÅVL)

CC makes this distinction along user caregories.
Non-Commercial = private individuals, public schools & education, public libraries, voluntary organizations, religious societies & secular denominations.

Basically, "Non-Commercial" means that there is no profit or at least that profit is not the main purpose of the user. To have a non-commercial status, the organization must be on the official national list of non-commercial entities.
